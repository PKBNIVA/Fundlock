Cookie Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how www.fundlock.in uses cookies and similar technologies.

What we use

  • Essential storage: we store your cookie choice in your browser so we don't ask again.
  • Application progress: what you type into the application form is kept in your browser's local storage so you can pick up where you left off, and unfinished applications are saved so our team can follow up. We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies.

Your choices

You can accept or decline cookies in the banner when you first visit. You can also clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. Declining does not affect your ability to apply.
