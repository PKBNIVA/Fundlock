Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

FundLock ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through www.fundlock.in, why we collect it, and your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Application details you submit: name, email, phone, company, city, sector, stage, funding needs, website, pitch deck link and business description.
  • Communications you send us by email or phone.
  • Usage data such as pages visited and device/browser type, collected through cookies if you accept them (see our Cookie Policy).

How we use it

  • To review your application and contact you about it.
  • With your consent, to share your startup profile with investors in our network who may be a fit.
  • To run the program: onboarding, mentoring, pitching sessions and the FundLock Conclave.
  • To improve our website and services.

Who we share it with

We share your profile with investors and ecosystem partners only as part of the program you applied for. We use service providers (for example Brevo for email and Vercel for hosting) who process data on our behalf. We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep application data for as long as needed to run the program and for any reapplication, and delete it on request unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, or raise a grievance by writing to info@fundlock.in. We will respond within the time required by law.
