    What the programme includes

    One programme, one fee, from application to term sheet.

    1. Warm introductions

      To investors who back your sector, stage and region. No cold lists.

    2. Pitch and deck review

      Honest feedback on your story, deck and financial model, and prep before every meeting.

    3. Investor sessions

      Pitch to several pre-screened investors in one sitting and hear their questions live.

    4. Term sheet support

      Help through due diligence, negotiation and closing.

    5. Grants and non-dilutive options

      Pointers to government grants, incubators and competitions that fit your business.

    6. Mentors and a founder cohort

      Founders who have raised before, service providers, and 29 other companies going through it with you.

    How it works

    About 30 days from application to onboarding, then roughly 30 days of fundraising.

    1. Days 1–3

      You apply

      Tell us about the business, your traction and what the money is for.

    2. Days 4–7

      We review

      We look at the business model, the market and whether the team can execute.

    3. Days 8–14

      Interview

      A conversation with our partners about your growth plan and capital needs.

    4. Days 15–21

      Due diligence

      We go through operations and financials so investors don't have to start from zero.

    5. Days 22–25

      Decision

      You hear from us either way. If it's a no, we tell you why.

    6. Days 26–30

      Onboarding and first pitches

      Your first investor sessions and an invitation to the FundLock Conclave.
